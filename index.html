CH01 — About

Where Biology
Meets the World

I am Anubhuti T. Shukla — PGT Biology, Head of Science Department, and CBSE Subject Matter Expert based in Delhi, India. I believe that every chapter in your NCERT textbook is secretly about climate change, war, geopolitics, or a pandemic.


ATS — Biology × World is my attempt to break the wall between the classroom and the crisis. Between the exam and the real emergency. Between what you memorise and what you must understand to survive the 21st century.


This space is for students who want to think deeper, teachers who want to teach wider, and anyone who believes science education should produce citizens — not just exam toppers.

CH05 — Lessons

Teaching Biology
as a World Event

Every lesson plan here is paired with a full article. Teachers get the classroom framework — students get the world context. Neither works without the other.

Class XI/XII · Ch.17 & Ch.13 — Respiratory System & Biodiversity · 2 Periods

Air Pollution as Political Crisis

Why does Delhi choke every winter — and who is biologically responsible? Students map PM2.5 from stubble fire to alveolus to policy failure.

Class XII · Ch.12 & Ch.13 — Ecosystem & Biodiversity · 2 Periods

When War Kills a River: The Euphrates

Connecting aquatic biome collapse to geopolitical conflict. Who controls the river — and what biology dies when one nation dams it?

Class XII · Ch.5 — Molecular Basis of Inheritance · 2 Periods

The Day the Central Dogma Cracked

From Crick's 1958 declaration to Drt3b's 2026 discovery — charting every crack in the most important rule in molecular biology.

Class XII · Ch.5 & Ch.9 — Molecular Biology & Biotechnology · 2 Periods

Drt3b: From Bacteria to Biotechnology

CRISPR was found in bacteria and became a Nobel Prize. Drt3b was just found in 2026. Students map the pathway from discovery to application.

Class XII · Ch.4 & Ch.5 — Inheritance & Molecular Biology · 3 Periods

Your Mother's Mitochondria: Maternal Inheritance & Three-Parent Babies

Tracing the unbroken maternal thread from Mitochondrial Eve to the UK's three-parent baby controversy to India's invisible mitochondrial disease crisis.

Global · IB / IGCSE Compatible · 3 Periods

Antibiotic Resistance as a Geopolitical Problem

Why pharmaceutical companies stopped developing new antibiotics — and what that means for global health security.
